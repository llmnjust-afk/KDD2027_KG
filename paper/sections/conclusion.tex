\section{Conclusion}
\label{sec:conclusion}

We presented \method{}, a training-free retrieval controller whose primary mechanism is marginal-gain early stopping, augmented by conditional adaptive-beam and use-graph levers. On multi-hop reasoning with a strong judge it improves both accuracy and cost over the fixed baseline (F1 $+10.3\%$, cost $-23.2\%$, $p{=}0.049$), on mixed-depth streams it outperforms trained routers (LogReg and MLP) by up to $14.7\%$ F1 without training data, and the cost saving transfers to a second KG (WebQSP/Freebase, $-19\%$). We report honest boundaries: a tuned fixed budget or MLP beats it on single-depth splits, F1 trades lower on denser KGs, and weak judges at depth make graph retrieval harmful. Future work: larger KGs, frontier models, calibrated judges.
