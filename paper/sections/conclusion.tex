\section{Conclusion}
\label{sec:conclusion}

We presented \method{}, a training-free retrieval controller whose primary mechanism is marginal-gain early stopping, augmented by conditional adaptive-beam and use-graph levers. On multi-hop reasoning with a strong judge it improves both accuracy and cost over the fixed baseline (F1 $+10.3\%$, cost $-23.2\%$, $p{=}0.049$), on mixed-depth streams it outperforms a trained router by $8.9\%$ F1 without training data, and the cost saving transfers to a second KG (WebQSP, $-15\%$). We report honest boundaries: a tuned fixed budget beats it on single-depth splits, and weak judges at depth make graph retrieval harmful. Future work: larger KGs, frontier models, calibrated judges.
